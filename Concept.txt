TribalMMO

Full Game Design Document

1. High-Level Overview

TribalMMO is a persistent online tribal-era RPG where players form player-run factions, build shared settlements, and shape the world through exploration, crafting, combat, diplomacy, and territory control. Progression is finite, choices are permanent, and the endgame is political and social, not numerical. Pets exist only as optional buff companions.

2. World & Setting

Nervella

A prehistoric fantasy continent defined by forests, plains, mountains, riverlands, ancient ruins, and spirit sites. The world is static but persistent, shaped entirely by player actions.

Environmental Systems

Weather: Rain, storms, and droughts affect exploration and gathering.

Seasons: Spring, summer, autumn, and winter affect resources and rituals.

Spirit Phenomena: Rare events granting temporary buffs.

3. Player Character

Character Creation

Players choose a name and background (flavor only).

Core Stats (Finite)

Stats cap at Tier III:

- Strength
- Agility
- Endurance
- Focus
- Cunning

Stats increase through skill tiers, equipment, rituals, and settlement bonuses.

4. Skills & Progression (Finite)

Skill Categories

Each skill has three tiers:

- Gathering
- Crafting
- Combat
- Survival
- Diplomacy

Tier Unlocks

Tier I to Tier II: Basic recipes, basic zones, and the ability to join factions.

Tier II to Tier III: Advanced recipes, access to ruins and spirit sites, the ability to found factions, and the ability to hold faction roles.

Specializations (Permanent)

Chosen at Tier II and Tier III:

- Smith
- Bonecarver
- Herbalist
- Fletcher
- Scout
- Raider
- Ritualist

5. Pet System (Optional)

Pets provide minor passive buffs and require minimal care. They do not fight or level infinitely.

Pet Types

- Hawk: +3% scouting yield
- Boar: +5% carrying capacity
- Lizard: +2% crafting speed
- Fox: +3% evasion
- Beetle: +2% herb gathering

6. Player-Run Factions

Founding a Faction

Requirements:

- Tier II skill
- Founding Rite quest
- Resource cost (wood, stone, hides)

Faction Identity

Founders choose a name, symbol, philosophy, and starting location.

Faction Philosophies

Each philosophy provides balanced bonuses:

- Warborn: +5% raid damage
- Earthkeepers: +10% building speed
- Pathfinders: +10% exploration yield
- Spiritseekers: +5% ritual success

Faction Roles

- Chieftain
- Warden
- Artisan Master
- Scout Captain
- Ritual Keeper

Faction Laws

Chieftains set tax rates, resource distribution, raid policies, and membership rules.

7. Settlement System

Each faction controls one main settlement.

Settlement Buildings (Tier I-III)

- Longhouse
- Barracks
- Workshop
- Garden
- Storage Pit
- Spirit Circle
- Watchtower

Settlement Progression

Upgrades require resources, time, and player contributions.

8. Territory Control

The world map is divided into hex territories.

Territory Types

- Forest
- Plains
- Mountains
- Riverlands
- Ruins
- Spirit Sites

Territory Actions

Factions can claim territory, build outposts, harvest resources, raid rivals, and defend territory.

Territory Bonuses

Resource yield, exploration access, spirit blessings, and defensive advantages.

9. Gameplay Loops

Exploration

Tap-forward movement triggering resource finds, encounters, weather effects, ruin events, and spirit phenomena.

Actions

Timed actions include:

- Crafting
- Training
- Building
- Scouting
- Rituals
- Diplomacy

Durations range from 5 minutes to 8 hours.

Combat

Turn-based, text-driven, deterministic combat including wild encounters, ruin guardians, faction skirmishes, territory raids, and PvP.

10. Spirit System

Spirits are environmental forces.

Spirit Types

Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Shadow, and Light.

Spirit Interactions

Rituals, offerings, and seasonal events.

Spirit Rewards

Temporary buffs, crafting bonuses, combat modifiers, and weather manipulation.

11. Economy & Crafting

Resource Types

Wood, stone, bone, clay, copper, tin, herbs, hides, and ritual components.

Crafting Professions

Smith, Bonecarver, Herbalist, Weaver, and Fletcher.

Crafting System

Timers, skill checks, quality tiers, and settlement bonuses.

Player Market

Auction house, faction marketplaces, and seasonal supply and demand.

12. Social Systems

Diplomacy

Alliances, wars, trade, and shared territory.

Communication

Faction chat, alliance chat, world announcements, and settlement boards.

Events

Seasonal festivals, spirit phenomena, territory conflicts, and ruin expeditions.

13. Monetization

Cosmetic-focused:

- Cosmetics
- Settlement skins
- Pet skins
- Convenience boosts
- Seasonal passes

14. Long-Term Extensibility

Future expansions:

- New continents
- Mounts
- Weather manipulation
- Spirit fusion rituals
- Player-run towns
- Clan wars
- Procedural ruins
- Seasonal story arcs

15. Summary

TribalMMO is a social, political, tribal-era RPG with finite progression and player-driven worldbuilding. Its strength lies in faction dynamics, settlement building, and territory control.
