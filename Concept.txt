TribalMMO

Polished High-Level Concept

TribalMMO is a persistent, tribal-era online RPG where players shape a shared world through exploration, crafting, combat, and clan society. Progression is infinite, choices are permanent, and the game has no traditional endgame — the world evolves entirely through player actions.

1. World & Setting

A static but fully persistent world inspired by early tribal civilizations.

Spirit Phenomena

Rare supernatural events that temporarily empower players or settlements. These moments create spikes of opportunity and encourage exploration and clan coordination.

2. Player Character

Players create a named character with four infinitely scaling core stats:

- Strength — physical power
- Defence — resilience
- Speed — agility
- Vitality — health

Stats grow through training but costs energy. (energy system needs redesign)

3. Skills & Progression

Skills provide finite progression (Level 1–100), unlocking efficiencies, recipes, and combat techniques.

Skill Categories

- Gathering
- Crafting
- Combat
- Woodcutting
- Mining

Player Level

A meta-level calculated from total skill progression. Used for matchmaking, unlocks, and more.

4. Pet System

Pets are loyal companions that offer passive core stat bonuses. They require no care, do not fight, and do not scale infinitely — they are identity and utility.

5. Player-Run Clans

Players can form clans to build settlements, coordinate raids, and shape regional politics.

Founding Requirements

- 5,000 Gold Coins (Temporarily 10 gold)

Clan Identity

- Name
- Description
- Optional banner/icon

Clan Roles

Leaders assign structured roles such as Chieftain, Elder, Hunter, Gatherer, Crafter, and Member.

Clan Laws

Chieftains define:
- Tax rates
- Resource distribution
- Raid permissions
- Membership rules

Clans form the backbone of TribalMMO's social and political landscape.

6. Settlement System

Each clan controls one main settlement, which evolves through player contributions.

Settlement Buildings (Tier I–III)

- Longhouse
- Barracks
- Workshop
- Garden
- Storage Pit
- Spirit Circle
- Watchtower

Upgrades require resources, time, and collective effort, reinforcing clan cooperation.

7. Core Gameplay Loops

Exploration

Tap-forward movement (Take a step) reveals:
- Resource nodes
- Wildlife encounters
- Ruins
- Spirit phenomena
- Random RNG events (chance of just random text like 'you tripped over a stone', chance of finding gold coin(s), chance of finding random item, etc)

Exploration is deterministic but unpredictable in outcome.

Combat

Turn-based, text-driven combat with deterministic resolution. Includes:
- Wild encounters
- Clan skirmishes
- Settlement raids
- PvP duels

Combat rewards resources, XP, and clan reputation.

8. Economy & Crafting

Resources

Wood, stone, bone, clay, copper, tin, herbs, hides

Crafting System

Crafting uses timers, skill checks, quality tiers, and settlement bonuses. Higher skill and better facilities yield superior items.

Player Market

- Global auction house
- Clan marketplaces with adjustable taxes

9. Social Systems

Diplomacy

Clans can form alliances, declare wars, negotiate trade, or remain neutral.

Communication

- Clan chat
- Private messages
- Forums
- World announcements

10. Monetization

A hybrid model:
- Subscription
- Optional in-app purchases for in-game currency
- No pay-to-win mechanics

11. Long-Term Extensibility

Future expansions may include:
- Mounts
- Clan war seasons
- Dynamic story arcs
- Advanced RNG systems
- New skills, and settlement features

12. Summary

TribalMMO is a social, tribal-era RPG built around infinite progression, clan identity, and a world shaped entirely by its players. Its strength lies in cooperative settlement building, deterministic combat, and meaningful social dynamics.
